What AI support resolves in a casino or sportsbook

AI support resolves the repetitive, data-driven questions that make up most of an iGaming support queue, and escalates the cases that need a human decision. The agent does not just quote an FAQ — it checks the player’s actual account, bonus progress or transaction status and answers with specifics.

  • Withdrawals: live status from the payment provider, expected arrival time, and a ticket opened automatically if a payout is stuck. More in our guide to automating deposit and withdrawal support.
  • Deposits that “didn’t arrive”: matched against PSP data, including p2p transfers where the payer’s name rarely matches the account; uploaded PDF receipts are checked for forgery.
  • KYC and verification: what is missing, which document to upload and where; accounts on a compliance hold are explained and routed with context.
  • Bonuses and wagering: how much is wagered, how much is left, which games count and at what rate — calculated from your bonus rules.
  • Limits, blocks and account access: handled by your own flows rather than routed to an operator.
  • Betting rules and settlement: answers from the rules you keep in the knowledge base; disputed settlements go to a human.

What stays with people: disputes, chargeback threats, fraud and risk signals, VIP exceptions and any sensitive situation such as responsible-gambling concerns. QODEQ escalates these to your team with the full conversation and player context, so nobody has to ask the player to repeat themselves.

How it works

Every request goes through the same pipeline: the agent gathers context from your systems, finds the applicable rule, and answers in your brand’s tone of voice — usually in about 7 seconds.

  1. The player writes from any channel — website, app or messenger. Thousands of conversations run at once, so no queue forms on match days or during promos.
  2. The AI pulls context from your CRM and platform before the first reply: KYC tier, account status, betting and bonus history.
  3. It checks the knowledge base — rules, bonuses, limits and KYC flows that your team updates without developers.
  4. It resolves or escalates. Payment questions go to the Payment Assistant inside the same chat; cases that need a decision go to an operator with context.
  5. Everything is logged: helpdesk tickets are created and enriched automatically, each dialogue is tagged for analytics, and the AI QA System can score the conversations your operators handle.

Why a specialised iGaming model, not a generic chatbot

A specialised model understands iGaming intent out of the box — wagering, KYC tiers, PSP statuses, p2p deposits, free bets — so it resolves requests instead of deflecting them to an article. General-purpose support bots can be configured for gambling, but the domain logic and integrations usually have to be built by the operator.

What players ask — and what the AI does

“Where is my withdrawal?”

The AI Payment Assistant reads the live transaction status from the payment provider and tells the player where the payout is and when it should arrive, in about 10 seconds. If the payout is stuck, it opens and enriches a ticket automatically.

“My deposit didn’t arrive.”

The AI matches the request against PSP data, including p2p transfers where the payer’s name does not match the account. Uploaded PDF receipts are checked for forgery before any manual action.

“Why can’t I withdraw my bonus winnings?”

The AI Chat Operator checks the player’s wagering progress against your bonus rules and explains exactly how much is left and which games count. No operator is involved.

“How long does verification take and what documents do I need?”

The AI reads the player’s KYC status and your verification flow, tells them what is missing and sends the upload link. Accounts on a compliance hold are escalated with context.

“My account is blocked.”

The AI explains the reason that applies — for example, a pending KYC step — and the next action. Blocks related to risk, fraud or compliance decisions go to your team.

“I want to close my account or take a break.”

Requests related to responsible gambling, such as self-exclusion or limits, follow your own procedures. Sensitive cases are handed to a human operator with the full conversation.
