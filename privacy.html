Privacy Policy

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you visit qodeq.net or contact us, why we collect it, who we share it with and how you can exercise your rights.

1. Who we are

The website qodeq.net is operated by QODEQ (“QODEQ”, “we”, “us”). QODEQ provides an AI support platform for iGaming operators. For this website we are the controller of your personal data.

For any privacy question or request, write to support [at] qodeq.net or message us on Telegram at @qodeq.

This policy covers the website only. Data that our clients process through the QODEQ platform (for example, conversations with their players) is processed on the client’s behalf under a separate data processing agreement.

2. What data we collect

Data you give us

When you send a request through a form on the site (a calculation request, a meeting request or the business case on /calculator), we receive:

  • your name;
  • your company name, if you enter it;
  • your email address, Telegram username or phone number;
  • the product you are interested in and the language of the page;
  • the figures you entered in a calculator, if you send them with your request.

If you write to us by email or Telegram, we receive the content of your message and your contact details.

Data collected automatically

  • Technical data sent with a request: IP address, browser and device type (user agent), the page you sent the request from, and the date and time.
  • Traffic source: UTM parameters and ad click IDs (gclid, fbclid, yclid) from the link you followed, the referring website and the first page you visited — so we know which channels bring requests. This is kept in your browser’s local storage (key qodeq_attrib_v1) for your first and most recent visit, and sent to us only together with a request.
  • Analytics: pages viewed, time on site, approximate location derived from your IP address, device and browser type, collected by Google Analytics 4 through cookies.
  • Security and delivery: our CDN provider Cloudflare processes your IP address and request data to deliver the site and protect it from attacks.

We do not ask for and do not want sensitive data (health, political views, payment card details and the like). Please do not send it through our forms.

4. Cookies and analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Ltd / Google LLC). It sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to tell visits apart; they are kept for up to 2 years. We use analytics only in aggregated form to understand how the site is used.

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on. The site works without analytics cookies.

5. Who receives your data

Your data is available to the QODEQ team members who handle requests. We also use service providers that process data on our behalf:

  • Cloud hosting provider — stores the website and the database of requests;
  • Cloudflare — content delivery and protection of the site;
  • Google — email delivery (Google Workspace / Gmail) and website analytics (Google Analytics);
  • Telegram — internal notifications about new requests to our sales team;
  • ZeroBounce — checking that the email address you enter in the form exists (only the address itself is sent).

We may disclose data if required by law or to protect our rights, and to a successor in the event of a merger or acquisition, subject to this policy.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Where this happens, we rely on safeguards required by law, such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

7. How long we keep data

  • Requests and correspondence — for as long as needed to handle your request and our business relationship, and no longer than 24 months after our last contact, unless the law requires longer.
  • Technical data used for spam protection — kept with the request it belongs to; rate-limiting data is held in memory for minutes.
  • Analytics data — up to 14 months in Google Analytics; cookies up to 2 years.

8. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you (including the GDPR), you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate data;
  • ask us to delete your data;
  • restrict or object to processing, including processing based on legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent;
  • lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

To exercise a right, write to support [at] qodeq.net. We reply within one month. We may ask you to confirm your identity before acting on a request.

9. Security

The site is served only over HTTPS. Access to the database of requests is limited to the people who need it, and our servers and accounts are protected with access controls. No system is completely secure, so please contact us right away if you believe your data has been compromised.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses. The site is not directed at people under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services or the law change. The date of the latest version is shown at the top of this page.
